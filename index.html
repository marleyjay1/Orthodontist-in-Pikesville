Orthodontic Treatments for Children and Adults

Every smile is different, which is why orthodontic treatment should be tailored to each patient. Depending on your dental condition, your orthodontist may recommend different options.

Traditional Braces

Traditional braces are a reliable option for correcting crowded teeth, gaps, crooked teeth, and bite problems. They use brackets and wires to gradually move teeth into their proper positions. Regular adjustments help keep treatment progressing as planned.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners offer a discreet alternative to traditional braces. These removable trays gradually move teeth into better alignment. They are popular among adults and teenagers who want a less noticeable orthodontic treatment option.

Early Orthodontic Treatment

Children can benefit from an orthodontic evaluation while their teeth and jaws are still developing. Early assessments may help identify potential alignment issues and determine whether treatment is needed.

Bite Correction

Problems such as overbites, underbites, crossbites, and open bites can affect both comfort and dental function. Orthodontic treatment can help improve bite alignment and create a healthier smile.

Signs You May Need Orthodontic Treatment

Not everyone recognises the signs of orthodontic problems. However, several common issues may indicate that you should schedule an orthodontic consultation.

  • Crooked or crowded teeth.
  • Noticeable gaps between teeth.
  • Overbite or underbite.
  • Difficulty chewing certain foods.
  • Teeth that do not meet properly when biting.
  • Jaw discomfort associated with bite problems.
  • Difficulty cleaning between crowded teeth.

If you notice any of these concerns, consulting an experienced orthodontic professional can help you understand your options.
